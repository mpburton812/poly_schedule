Create an app that is a polyamory event and sleeping schedule manager, leveraging google calendar as a back-end that enables async schedule planning proposals, The app should allow for async scheduling proposals, reviews, and agreements for attending events and sleeping schedules. The app should have a modern, interesting UI that presents out the current schedule for the week both for events and sleeping arrangements. The app should be built to conform to Android Material Design standards.

To accomplish this, the app should have a way to configure the following: individual people for their default home, homes and available bedrooms in that home, logistics between people including who they sleep with and their preferred minimum and maximum nights to be with that person (if a proposal is outside the preferred sleeping nights, it will notify the proposer prior to send and the reviewer(s) as part of their review). 

Additionally, a user should be able to make an event proposal to any number of people in the poly circle. 

Additionally, a user should be able to make a sleeping proposal for either a single night or multiple nights to any number of people in the poly circle. 

Additionally, all users should receive a proposal if they are involved and accept or reject with comments. 

Additionally, the proposal system should be graphical, showing the schedule proposal in the context of the rest of the poly schedule. 

Additionally, the person who made the proposal should be notified when their proposal is reviewed.

Additionally, the person who made the proposal should be able to cancel or retract or modify and re-send the proposal based on review responses. 

Additionally, the person who made the proposal should be able to see all responses in a unified view. 

Additionally, when a proposal for either an even or sleeping arrangement is approved by all approvers, it should update the google calendar with that information.

Additionally, the app should allow scheduling on behalf of people who are not using the app. 

Additionally, the app should plug into the phone notification system and allow notifications when new proposals are available.
